Software that fixes itself.

It lives on your box, watches production and hunts bugs.

Each one becomes a trail: evidence, repro, patch.

It proves each fix. You approve what matters.

It lands on Radicle, Tangled or plain git.

Good fixes set. Bad ones undo themselves.

Jellyware: soft enough to change, firm enough to trust.
